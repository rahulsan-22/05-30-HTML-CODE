Shadow      
              of 
              Love

QSpiders Dilsukhnagar lo Java batch ki join ayina iddaru... Arjun mariyu Meghana. Modati roju nundi vallaki oka special bonding start ayyindi. Morning classes, tea breaks, mock interviews... ila rojulu gadusthu valliddaru premalo padipoyaru. Oka roju Meghana sudden ga classes ki raaledu. Phone switch off. Messages ki reply ledu. Arjun ki edo tappu jarigindani anipinchindi. Rendu rojula tarvata Meghana vachindi... kani ame behaviour complete ga maaripoyindi. Smile ledu... matladatam takkuva... eppudu bayapadutune undedi. Arjun investigation start chesadu. Oka night, Meghana ni secret ga follow ayyadu. Ame Dilsukhnagar lo unna oka old warehouse daggara ki vellindi. Akkada konthamandi unknown persons tho meet ayyindi. Arjun hidden ga video record chestunte... oka shocking nijam bayataki vachindi. Meghana father oka financial fraud case lo key witness. Aa criminals Meghana ni blackmail chestu, confidential files collect cheyyamani force chestunnaru. Aa gang leader ki Arjun record chestunnadu ani telisipoyindi. "Phone ivvu... lekapothe ikkade champestham," ani warning icharu. Kani Arjun courage tho escape ayi, evidence motham police ki ichadu. Next day police operation start ayyindi. Warehouse meeda raid jarigindi. Heavy chase tarvata criminals arrest ayyaru. Case close ayyaka Meghana kannullo neellu tho, "Ninnu dooram pettadam ninnu kapadadanike... kani nuvvu naa kosam praanam risk chesavu," ani cheppindi. Arjun navvuthu, "Prema ante pakkana nilabadatam kaadu... danger lo unna sare vadilipokapovadam," ani annadu. Konni nelala tarvata valliddaru software jobs sampadincharu. Ade QSpiders Dilsukhnagar mundu nilabadi oka photo teesukoni Meghana cheppindi, "Mana love story ikkade start ayyindi... crime tho test ayyindi... trust tho gelichindi." Arjun ame cheyyi pattukoni navvadu. The End.
